# ACOS Control Plane Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

ACOS Control Plane is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all community members, regardless of:
- Age
- Body size
- Disability
- Ethnicity
- Gender identity and expression
- Level of experience
- Nationality
- Personal appearance
- Race
- Religion
- Sexual identity and orientation

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Supporting and encouraging others

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation, or bullying (online or offline)
- Discriminatory remarks or jokes
- Unwelcome sexual or romantic attention
- Personal attacks or insults
- Publishing private information without consent
- Derailing conversations or disrupting discussions
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above behaviors

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all spaces associated with the ACOS Control Plane project, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions
- Issue tracking systems
- Email communications
- Team meetings and video calls
- Conferences and meetups
- Social media accounts
- Any public representation of the project

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it:

**Email:** conduct@acos.dev
**Confidential:** Yes - Reports are kept confidential
**Response Time:** Reports will be reviewed within 48 hours

When reporting, include:
- Description of the incident
- When and where it occurred
- Who was involved (if known)
- Your contact information
- Any supporting evidence

### Response & Investigation

The ACOS Core Team will:

1. Acknowledge receipt of the report
2. Conduct a fair and impartial investigation
3. Consider all relevant information
4. Maintain confidentiality to the greatest extent possible
5. Respond within 7 days with findings
6. Take appropriate corrective action

### Possible Outcomes

Depending on the severity and nature of the violation:

- Private warning or correction
- Public warning or apology
- Temporary ban from community spaces
- Permanent ban from community spaces
- Referral to relevant authorities (if applicable)

## Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in temporary or permanent removal from community participation, at the discretion of the ACOS Core Team.

## Appeal Process

If you believe an enforcement decision was unfair:

1. Submit an appeal to conduct@acos.dev within 7 days
2. Provide additional context or evidence
3. A different team member will review the appeal
4. The review will be completed within 14 days
